How should I compare creative subscriptions?

Compare what you can make, not just a credit number. Video length, resolution, model version, and generation mode all affect usage. A still, a five-second clip, and a game-building session are different workloads. Check the allowance for your actual workflow before choosing a plan.

Before you start.

Do I need to know how to use AI tools?+

Start with an idea, drawing, script, or reference. The agent-led approach is designed to help you develop the direction step by step. You stay in control of the creative decisions.

Can I build a game here?+

You can develop the visual direction for characters, environments, vehicles, and cinematics now. The planned Blender-to-game workflow, Unity and Unreal connections, and our own Arcade Engine are coming soon. Playable game generation and exports are not available yet.

Are all models included in every plan?+

Check the live studio for the current model catalog, credit allowances, and plan limits before purchasing. This page does not promise unlimited generation or universal model access.

Can I move between styles and formats?+

Use your starting material to explore different visual directions, from anime-inspired character work to cinematic images and motion. Results depend on the selected model and your references.
